Junction 1 · Spend

It cannot run away with your money.

Every job carries a hard ceiling on spend, on time, and on steps. The runtime checks that ceiling before each action it takes. When the ceiling is reached, the job stops, mid-stride if it must.

The worst case is a number you chose.

  steps: 40

Junction 2 · Boundary

It cannot see anything you didn't show it.

You point it at one folder. That folder is the whole world the job can see.

The AI is never asked where the line is, so there is nothing to negotiate with. When it proposes reading outside the folder, the runtime refuses, and the refusal itself goes on the record.

Junction 5 · Cost

The same work, for less, decided by you.

Send the routine steps to a fast, inexpensive model and save the careful one for the moment of judgment. The route is written in the job's own file. The AI has no say in it.

Nothing is welded to one vendor, either. If pricing changes or a model is retired, you change one word.
