Junction 1 · Spend

It cannot run away with your money.

Every job carries a hard ceiling — on spend, on time, on steps. The ceiling is checked before every single action the AI takes, so a task can never quietly run up a bill or hang forever.

The worst case is a number you chose.

◌ the budget block — four lines in the job's own file

budget:
  tokens: 20000
  time_seconds: 120
  steps: 40

Junction 2 · Boundary

It cannot see anything you didn't show it.

You point it at one folder. It cannot reach outside — and it cannot talk its way out, because the boundary is not something the AI is ever consulted about.

Anything it proposes that crosses the line is refused, and the refusal itself goes on the record.

◌ the whole boundary, in one line

scope: ..

◌ a refusal, on the record

reason.step · action_rejection
rejected 1 proposed action: tool not granted

Junction 3 · Evidence

You can see exactly what it did.

Every run writes a complete, ordered record: what the AI was asked, what it opened, what it decided, what it produced. The record prints as plain English — something you can hand to a client or a manager, not a wall of JSON.

When a number is an estimate rather than a measurement, the record says so.

Junction 4 · Proof

You can prove it again, later, without paying again.

The record is not a description of the work — it is a re-runnable copy of it. Any past job can be re-executed from its recording and verified, on a machine with no AI vendor account at all.

Record once. Replay anywhere.

◌ the verification block, printed after every replay

replay: executed
output_matches: True
completions: 3/3 used, 0 prompt divergence(s)

Junction 5 · Cost

The same work, for less, decided by you.

Route the routine steps to a fast, inexpensive model and keep the expensive one for the step that actually needs it — set in the job's own file, never decided by the AI.

And nothing is welded to one vendor. If pricing changes or a model is retired, you change one word.

◌ the same job, two vendors — one changed word

model:
  provider: openai
model:
  provider: gemini

Junction 6 · Compounding

It gets better because you used it.

Every job leaves behind a complete, self-contained record of how your operation actually works — owned by you, not by an AI vendor. That accumulating record is what the system learns your work from.

The network under the tree was never plumbing. It was the asset, growing the whole time.
